About Suvarna River Prediction System

v2.4.0-web

Institution & Research Lab

National Institute of Technology Karnataka (NITK Surathkal)
Department of Applied Mechanics and Hydraulics
Remote Sensing & Coastal GIS Laboratory
Study Area: Kodi Bengre, Udupi District, Karnataka, India

Scientific Objective

Quantifying multi-decadal river-mouth channel migration, sand spit breaching, and inlet path deviations for the Suvarna-Sita estuarine system using historical satellite imagery (1989-2026) and deep learning morphodynamic models.

Satellite Data Sources

• Landsat 5 TM (1989-2011)
• Landsat 7 ETM+ (1999-2022)
• Landsat 8/9 OLI/TIRS (2013-2026)
• Copernicus Sentinel-2 MSI (10m Resolution)
• Coordinate System: EPSG:32643 (WGS 84 / UTM zone 43N)

Methodology & Architecture

• Automated Water Masking via Modified NDWI (MNDWI)
• Throat Centroid & Boundary Deviation Calculation
• Euclidean Displacement & Cardinal Bearing Estimation
• 4-Class LULC Surface Area Quantification
• Monsoonal Runoff & Climate Correlation
